# Milestone 3: Data Mining Techniques, Model Development, and Video Presentation

Duration: 4 weeks

This milestone focuses on applying data mining techniques to analyze and extract meaningful patterns from your dataset. You are encouraged to experiment with different approaches, refine your models, and document your findings comprehensively. You will submit a written report and a group video presentation summarizing your work.

## Weeks 1–2: Group Formation and Data Preparation

    Use the Milestone 3 Group Discussion forum to discuss your dataset and form groups of 2 to 3 people.
    Distribute tasks among the group members.
    Perform preprocessing on the data as needed, including:
        Handling missing values
        Encoding categorical variables (e.g., one-hot encoding)
        Scaling numerical features
        Feature extraction
    Conduct deeper analysis of feature distributions and address class imbalance if applicable.

## Week 3: Model Development and Experimentation

    Apply one or more data mining techniques:
        Supervised learning (classification/regression)
        Unsupervised learning (clustering, anomaly detection)
        Association rule mining
        Text mining (TF-IDF or other techniques)
        Deep Learning model
    Train and evaluate multiple models using appropriate metrics.
    Perform hyperparameter tuning (e.g., grid search, random search) if applicable.

## Week 4: Interpretation, Report, and Video Presentation

    Interpret model results using visualizations and statistical measures.
    Compare your results with previous work and highlight your unique contributions.
    Document the final methodology and findings in a structured report.
    Record a group video presentation of the project and include its link in the report.

## Deliverables
1. Report

The report must contain:

    Your project name, group members, and a clear distribution of work among the members
    Knowledge about the data: variable types, feature distributions, and possible problems with the data (high correlation among features, missing values, need for feature transformation, etc.)
    Pre-processing steps and results
    Results of model experiments, including evaluation metrics
    Justification for model choices and optimizations
    Interpretation of findings
    A link to executable code (.ipynb file) ensuring reproducibility
    A link to your group's video presentation

2. Video Presentation

Create a video presentation summarizing your group project, covering your work, methods, results, and individual contributions.

Content

    The video must not exceed 30 minutes.
    Include the following:
        An introduction explaining the project's purpose, objectives, and dataset
        A discussion of your pre-processing steps, methods, and modeling approach
        A presentation of your results, including evaluation metrics and model comparisons
        A conclusion summarizing lessons learned and potential next steps

Team participation

    All group members must actively participate in the video.
    Each member must state their specific contributions to the project.

Visual aids

    Use slides, charts, graphs, or screen recordings of your notebook to support your explanation.
    Make sure all visuals are clear and relevant.

Technical requirements

    Upload the video to a hosting platform (e.g., YouTube, Vimeo, or Google Drive).
    Set the privacy to "unlisted" or "anyone with the link can view."
    Test the link before submission (e.g., in a private/incognito browser window) to confirm it is accessible. An inaccessible video cannot be graded.

## Submission

    Each group member must submit the report.
    The report must include working links to both the executable code and the video presentation.

## Grading Guidelines

| Criteria | Weight | Description |
|----------|--------|-------------|
| Data Preparation & Feature Engineering | 20% | Completeness of preprocessing, feature selection, and handling of class imbalance. | 
| Model Development & Experimentation | 40% | Depth of experimentation, evaluation of multiple models, and effectiveness of tuning. |
| Interpretation & Report Quality | 20% | Clarity, depth, and originality of insights, comparisons, and justifications. |
| Video Presentation | 20% | Depth and clarity of the project explanation (16%), audio/video quality and professionalism (2%), and adherence to the time limit (2%). |

Total: 100%
